The Story — What Problem Does This Solve?

Before · Manual
😩

A tedious chore

A speeding ticket arrives for a company car. Someone must open the PDF, squint at the plate number, search the staff list, and write an email to the right driver. Every single time.

→
The Idea
🤖

Teach a robot the routine

We wrote the routine down as a recipe: “WHEN a ticket lands in the folder, READ it, FIND the driver, SEND the mail.” A software robot now follows that recipe 24/7.

→
After · Automatic
☕

Done in ~7 seconds

Drop the ticket in a shared folder — that’s the only human action. The driver has the notice in their inbox before you finish your sip of coffee. No typos, nothing forgotten.

💡

What is “Power Automate”? (30-second version)

It is a Microsoft tool that lets you give a computer a simple instruction sheet: WHEN this happens → DO these steps. No programming degree needed — you snap together ready-made blocks like “watch this folder”, “read text from a document” (that part is the AI Hub, the robot’s eyes) and “send an email”. Think of it as a very reliable intern who never sleeps.
